Who sees which album

no ticks = nobody but you

Tick the people who may see an album. Everyone else stops seeing it — the album, its photographs, the map pin, the search results, and the direct image address. An album with nothing ticked is seen by nobody but you. Administrators are not in the list: they see every album anyway.
