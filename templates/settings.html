Which group means what

The names come from your identity provider. Somebody in an admin group sees and manages everything; a viewer may look; a contributor may add photographs. Several names per line, separated by commas.

the addresses your proxy talks to this site from — normally just 127.0.0.1

⚠ You cannot take away your own way back in: a change that would leave you not an administrator, or that drops the address this request came from, is refused and nothing is written.

A session carries the groups it had at sign-in. Change someone's groups {% if auth.proxy %}in your provider{% else %}here{% endif %} and they must sign out and back in — that is why both lines above are lists and not single names.
